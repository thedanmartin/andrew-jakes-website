Website Summary

This website focuses on a point in civilization where humanity is replaced by AI and machines where AI controls and dominates us. The purpose of this website is to promote awareness of a totalitarian AI takeover. We should not be controlled by AI, especially in a seemingly "Utopian" (Dystopian) state.

The objective is not to push us completely against AI, but it is something to keep us aware of the consequences if its presence resembles an existential threat. AI can and is being used for good, but this is an example of it heading in the wrong direction.

If such an event were to occur, we should be united us as human species against the creation an unpredictable intelligence greater than us. Our hasty curiosity may lead us to our extinction.
